# Privacy Policy – Mentis Publisher

Mentis Publisher is a private automation tool used only by the Mentis team (@bethementis)
to publish its own content to its own Instagram, Threads, YouTube and TikTok accounts.

- It accesses only the team's own accounts, with the team's explicit authorization.
- It uploads content the team has created and reads basic channel information needed to publish.
- It does not collect, store, sell or share personal data of any other user.
- Access tokens are stored as encrypted GitHub Actions secrets and are never logged.
